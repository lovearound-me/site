How it works, and what to check

lovearound does one thing: you tap a person, and they feel that someone is thinking of them. Here is how to use it, and what to look at when something doesn’t happen.

How it works

  • Add your people. Everyone has a Love ID: a link. Send yours to someone close, or open theirs, and give them a name only you will see.
  • Tap a person. A heart goes to them: their phone shows a notification, the edges of their screen glow, or a lamp on their shelf lights up.
  • That is all they get. A heart has no text and never says who it is from. They only know that someone who has them in their list thought of them just now.
  • Someone out there. Once an hour you can also send a heart to a random person somewhere in the world. They won’t know who it was, and you won’t know who got it.

A heart didn’t arrive

  • On an iPhone, check that notifications are allowed: Settings → Notification in the app, and Notifications in the iPhone’s own Settings. Without them you see hearts only while the app is open.
  • A pause or quiet hours may be on: Settings → Pause, and quiet hours under Settings → Who can reach it. Hearts sent meanwhile are not kept for later: a heart is now or never.
  • “Get hearts on this iPhone” may be off: Settings → What it does.
  • Whoever sends needs the other in their list. To send someone a heart, add them by their Love ID; they don’t have to add you back. To get hearts from them, give them yours.
  • On a computer the app has to be running: look for the heart in the menu bar or the tray.

Hearts from people I don’t know

Those are “someone out there” hearts: bare hearts like any other, at most one an hour, with nothing in them but the heart. If you’d rather not get them, turn them off in Settings → Who can reach it. Hearts from your own people keep coming.

If someone who has you in their list sends more hearts than you’d like, a pause silences everything for as long as you choose. We can’t tell you who it is: the server doesn’t keep that.

Signing in

With Apple, with Google, or with an email and a password. If the app says there is already an account with your email, sign in the way you did the first time; you can then add other ways in Settings → Account → Sign-in methods. A watch signs in with Apple or with a code you confirm on your phone.

Lamps, toys and your own boards

Anything that can light up or has a button can join: see the docs. A device is connected and disconnected in the Devices tab, where you also choose what it may do.

Deleting your account

Settings → Account → Delete account, in any of the apps or at app.lovearound.me. Your people, devices and the account are removed at once and can’t be brought back. What we keep until then is on the privacy page.
